History & Background

Tamil Nadu has one of the oldest civilizations, dating back over 2,000 years. The Chola, Chera, Pandya, and Pallava dynasties flourished here. The Chola Empire was one of the longest-ruling dynasties.

Tamil is one of the world's oldest living languages. The modern state was formed on January 26, 1950.

Culture & Lifestyle

The state is rich in classical arts, including Bharatanatyam dance and Carnatic music. It is famous for ancient temples featuring distinct Dravidian architecture.

Cuisine includes Idli, Dosa, Sambar, Chettinad dishes, and filter coffee. Major festivals include Pongal and various temple festivals. Chennai is often called the 'Detroit of India' due to its automobile industry.

Interesting Facts

  • Economy: Second-largest economy among Indian states.
  • Heritage: Brihadeeswara Temple is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Language: Tamil language has over 2,000 years of literary tradition.
  • Geography: Kanyakumari is the southernmost tip of mainland India.
