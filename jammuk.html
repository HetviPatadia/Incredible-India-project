History & Background

J&K has a complex history dating back thousands of years and was ruled by various dynasties. The Dogra dynasty ruled from 1846 to 1947.

On August 5, 2019, it was reorganized into two union territories: J&K and Ladakh. The region is historically known for its breathtaking natural beauty.

Culture & Lifestyle

The region has a rich cultural heritage with influences from Hindu, Muslim, and Buddhist traditions. It is famous for Kashmiri handicrafts, including Pashmina shawls and carpets.

Cuisine includes delicacies like Rogan Josh, Yakhni, Dum Aloo, and Kahwa tea. The region is also known for its soulful Sufi music. Tourism centers around Dal Lake and scenic valleys.

Interesting Facts

  • Title: Often called 'Paradise on Earth'.
  • Agriculture: Saffron from Pampore is world-renowned.
  • Spirituality: Vaishno Devi is one of India's most visited pilgrimage sites.
  • Sports: Gulmarg has one of the world's highest golf courses.
