History & Background

Known as the 'Land of Kings,' Rajasthan has a glorious history of Rajput warriors and kingdoms. Major kingdoms included Mewar, Marwar, Amber, and Jaipur. The region was known for its brave warriors who fought against foreign invasions.

The state was formed on March 30, 1949, by merging various princely states. Today, it stands as a testament to India's royal heritage with its magnificent forts and palaces.

Culture & Lifestyle

Rajasthan is rich in folk traditions including Ghoomar and Kalbelia dances. The state is famous for its colorful turbans, traditional jewelry, and intricate handicrafts. The region is also known for puppet shows, folk music, and vibrant festivals.

Cuisine plays a major role in the lifestyle here. Famous dishes include Dal Baati Churma, Laal Maas, Ker Sangri, and the sweet dish Ghevar.

Interesting Facts

Some interesting points about this destination:

  • Geography: Largest state in India by area.
  • Nature: The Thar Desert is India's largest desert.
  • Cities: Jaipur is known as the 'Pink City'.
  • Heritage: Has the highest concentration of forts and palaces in India.
