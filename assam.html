History & Background

Assam has a rich history dating back to the Kamarupa kingdom. The Ahom dynasty ruled for 600 years (1228-1826). The state played a crucial role in stopping Mughal expansion.

Tea cultivation was introduced by the British, transforming the region's economy. Assam became a full state on January 26, 1950.

Culture & Lifestyle

Assam is known for the Bihu festival, which is celebrated three times a year. The state is famous for its unique Assamese silk (Muga, Eri, Pat) and intricate bamboo crafts.

The cuisine includes rice-based dishes, fish curry, and the world-famous Assam tea. Traditional dance forms include the energetic Bihu dance and the classical Sattriya. The state is home to diverse ethnic communities.

Interesting Facts

  • Economy: Largest tea-producing state in India.
  • Wildlife: Kaziranga National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Conservation: Home to two-thirds of the world's one-horned rhinoceros.
  • Geography: Majuli is the world's largest river island.
